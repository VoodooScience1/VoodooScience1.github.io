Why Solution Architecture

I’m developing into Solution Architecture because it’s the natural extension of what I’ve been doing in practice for years: taking messy cross-functional problems and turning them into clear systems, decisions, and delivery paths that hold up under scrutiny.

SA lets me focus on what I enjoy most:
  • End-to-end design and trade-offs
  • Working across technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Security, safety, and governance as first-class constraints
  • Proving decisions with real artefacts, not just opinions
  • Developing efficiency, repeatability, scalability in a cost-conscious manner
