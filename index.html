Why SA?

I decided to develop into Solution Architecture because I believe it’s the natural next step from what I’ve been doing for years, in practice: taking messy, cross-functional problems and turning them into clear systems, frameworks, and delivery paths that actually work.

I’ve spent my career sitting at the junction of engineering, delivery, bids, risk, stakeholders, data, and governance; often being the person who has to connect the dots, simplify the chaos, and make decisions that hold up under scrutiny.

SA lets me lean into the bits I enjoy most - designing end-to-end solutions, thinking in trade-offs, looking at the bigger picture, building with security and safety at the core, and proving it with real artefacts.
