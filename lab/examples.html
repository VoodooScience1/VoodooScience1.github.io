Case Study / Portfolio Grid

Allows you to add a short/large intro, that can have whatever content you like. Then underneath this, a filterable searchable card grid for showcasing things.

The round icons top right provide links out to:

  • repos
  • websites
  • socials
  • a bespoke image gallery (that is project specific, and editable/updatable)
As well as a tag line, category, some space for text, and dates for the project. New custom tags can be added as you like. And managed as necessary. There's no limit to cards, and you can choose to show as may as you like (with a filed for choosing).
By default the cards are ordered from oldest to newest. Thre's no toggle/option to change this at the moment.
